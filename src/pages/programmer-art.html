Programmer art

Programmer art is the placeholder a developer draws when nobody else is going to. Grey boxes, a capsule with a face, proportions that are wrong in a way the author stopped seeing on day two. It ships more often than anyone admits, and it is affectionate as an insult, because the alternative was no art at all.

I did not draw these either. I described them and reviewed what came back, which makes them programmer art AI-assisted programmer art: a genre about eighteen months old, with its own tells. It is competent now, and confidently on-model, and it has no idea which of the four versions it just made is the funny one. That part is still the job. I picked these two out of a pile and threw the rest away.

Which is the argument the rest of this site makes, in its most literal available form. The drawing got cheap. Knowing which drawing was worth keeping did not.

The next one in the set is this page: a person explaining to a robot what programmer art is, and the robot drawing it correctly on the first try, which ruins the joke and proves it at the same time. I have not made that one yet. When I do it goes here.

Nothing else on this site has a picture on it, on purpose. This page is where that rule gets to be broken, and the mismatch is the point: it reads like a different site because it came out of a different part of the same workflow.
